Who we are

One team, two markets

MiS is a multidisciplinary team of power sector specialists, bringing together key experts with complementary backgrounds and expertise across the entire project lifecycle.

We support investors, project developers, EPC contractors, and technology providers throughout the renewable energy value chain – from project development and feasibility assessment to grid connection, commercial operation, and investment transactions involving operating assets, with a focus on identifying opportunities to optimise asset performance and minimise operating costs.

Our vision

To be a trusted strategic partner that connects resources and advances sustainable energy investment in Vietnam and Thailand.

The steps that decide COD in Vietnam

Grid connection, energisation, electricity operating licence and COD/CCA recognition with EVN, NSMO, MoIT and provincial authorities.

Control and dispatch infrastructure

SCADA, PPC, EMS, OCC and DCS for power plants and substations, designed to what system operators require.

Life after COD

Plant operation, O&M contracts, and revenue risk under PPA and tariff mechanisms.

The investor's side

Financing, M&A, land, E&S, carbon and I-REC.
