COD-ready Coordination

Grid connection approval, energisation and testing, electricity operating licence, COD and CCA recognition.

You receive: milestone roadmap, submissions, tracking until COD is recognised.

Owners · EPC · foreign PMC
PACKAGE 3

Grid & Control Review

Review of HV/MV design, SCADA/PPC/EMS and integrated control systems; FAT and SAT; grid code compliance; power system studies with our partners.

You receive: review reports, comment lists, compliance matrix.

EPC · foreign PMC/OE
PACKAGE 4

Owner's Advisor

Project governance, stage gates and board reporting; EPC, OEM and O&M contract strategy; interface and claims management.

You receive: an owner-side coordinator through each stage.

Owners · foreign PMC
PACKAGE 5

Technical Due Diligence

For operating plants: document register, technical gap review, SCADA/PPC/EMS and metering survey, curtailment outlook, PPA and tariff revenue risks.

You receive: ranked issue list with indicative cost and time to fix.

Buyers · sellers · transaction advisers

Also available with a package: project company set-up (with law firms), post-COD operation support, carbon, I-REC and ITMO, and local administrative support for companies new to Vietnam.
